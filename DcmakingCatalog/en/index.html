About Us

Doors Control Making is a young and dynamically developing company specializing in the development and production of electronic locking systems for doors used in clean rooms of the pharmaceutical, chemical, food and microelectronic industries.

The initial idea of creating our electronic devices was reliability and ease of installation. In the process of developing our devices, many similar products were studied and various problems in their operation were eliminated. One of the main principles of our company is an impeccable reputation in the market, achieved through absolute openness and honesty in working with partners.

Our company is constantly looking for ways to improve its products and develop new directions in door control devices. Pricing is also important: our prices differ significantly from the market for similar products, thanks to a competent approach and modern production technologies. We are ready for long-term cooperation and quality service.
